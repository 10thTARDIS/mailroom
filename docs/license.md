## Why the AGPL

The choice was deliberate ([ADR-011](adr/ADR-011-license.md)). Mailroom exists
because every capable AI-triage mail product is cloud SaaS that sends your mail
somewhere else; the AGPL is what keeps a hosted fork from quietly becoming that.

- **If you run a modified Mailroom as a network service for other people**,
  those users are entitled to your source.
- **If you self-host it for yourself, your family or your team**, the licence
  asks nothing of you. That is the case this project is built for.
