# Settings map

Every section of Settings in one place, with where to read more. Open Settings from the sidebar. Use **Find a setting** at the top to search by name or value.

Each section shows what it applies to: your whole **login**, one **account**, one **area**, or this **web** app.

| Section | What it is for | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Accounts** | Add, pause, reconnect or remove a mail account; account colour; per-folder syncing; **Ingest only** mode | [Getting started](getting-started.md#add-a-mail-account) |
| **Areas and drawers** | Your areas, what feeds them, drawer periods, learned sorting, day-one setup, the category tabs | [Desk and areas](desk-and-areas.md), [Sorting](sorting.md#learned-sorting) |
| **The line** | Where the line is, with a preview of its effect | [Desk and areas](desk-and-areas.md#move-the-line) |
| **In-tray** | How long before the tray nags; **Follow-up reminders** | [Desk and areas](desk-and-areas.md#the-in-tray) |
| **Rules** | Create, order, preview and apply rules | [Sorting](sorting.md#rules) |
| **Identities and signatures** | Your sending addresses, signatures and templates | [Writing](writing.md#signatures-and-templates) |
| **Notifications** | Push on this device, what notifies you, **Quiet hours**, which accounts notify | [Getting started](getting-started.md#turn-on-notifications) |
| **Senders** | VIPs and blocked senders | [Contacts](contacts-and-calendar.md#vips-and-blocking) |
| **Out of office** | Automatic reply, per account | [Contacts and calendar](contacts-and-calendar.md#out-of-office) |
| **Clean up** | Find senders to unsubscribe from, archive or block | [Sorting](sorting.md#clean-up-old-senders) |
| **Reading and safety** | **Senders whose images load** | [Reading safely](reading.md#remote-images) |
| **Keys** | Your PGP keys and your correspondents' keys | [Encryption](encryption.md) |
| **API keys and AI tools** | Keys, connected apps, approvals | [AI assistants](ai-assistants.md) |
| **Signed-in devices** | Where you are signed in; **Sign out** a device or **Sign out all other devices** | Below |
| **Activity** | What happened to your mail and access | [AI assistants](ai-assistants.md#see-what-happened) |
| **Saved searches** | Edit or delete smart folders | [Search](search.md#saved-searches) |
| **Appearance** | **Theme**, **Density**, **Text size**, **Rows**, **Reading pane**, **Message opens** | [Getting started](getting-started.md#choose-a-theme) |
| **Data** | Export and deletion of everything in your login | Below |

**Keyboard shortcuts** is a button at the top of Settings; see [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md). **Sign out** is in Settings too.

## Signed-in devices

Lists each browser or app signed in to your login. If you see one you do not recognise, choose **Sign out**, and change your password with your sign-in provider too. Ending sessions at the provider does not sign Mailroom devices out.

## Data

Exporting or deleting everything in your login is done by your operator, not from Settings.

## Account health

Open the account switcher; **Account health** is listed there. It shows, per account, whether syncing is **Healthy**, **Needs attention** or **Not syncing**, plus delivery failures, stuck changes and **Sends today**.
